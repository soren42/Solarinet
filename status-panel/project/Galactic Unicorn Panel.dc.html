Load spectrum and throughput

distribution histogram · 15-sample ribbon

The left two thirds is a histogram of the whole fleet by CPU load — 35 buckets from idle to saturated, column height being the share of systems in that bucket. A healthy fleet is a single soft hill in the middle; a saturated one leans right into amber and red, and a bimodal fleet shows two hills, which is the shape that tells you a pool is mis-sized. Bar height is a fraction, never a count, so the picture holds at any scale. The right block is aggregate link throughput — ingress above the line, egress below, amber where utilisation passes 85%.

Worst-first strip and instrument

severity quantiles · pool key · perf line

The recommendation. 2C

Hive field

texture grain is fleet size · warmth is load

The ambient one, made scale-aware: the grain of the field is set by the log of the fleet size, so a small estate reads as slow broad swells and a large one as fine cellular texture — the panel looks bigger when the fleet is bigger. Warmth is mean load, drifting from azure toward amber as the footprint heats up, and each outage is a discrete red spark that pulses over the field, capped at nine before the count takes over. Text cycles system count, mean load and aggregate throughput.

Turn 1 · Ambient display · Galactic unicorn

SolariNet at a glance, on 583 LEDs

Four candidate faces for the panel. Each one holds the quiet-healthy principle — the calm majority stays a dim lattice, and amber, red and violet are the only things that pull the eye across a room. Scenario, brightness and diffuser apply to all four so they can be compared honestly.

53 × 11 grid · 583 LEDs · 6 mm pitch · 330 mm wide · RP2350 at 150 MHz, ~300 Hz refresh, 14-bit · fleet data arrives over USB serial from the UNO Q

Driven by the controls at the top of the page.

Lattice and ledger

one LED per system · fixed counts

The whole fleet as a 29-column lattice, one LED per system, ordered by pool. Healthy systems breathe at a fifth brightness; a fault is the only bright thing on the board. The right ledger holds the two numbers an operator actually needs, so nothing has to be counted by eye.

Pool bars and ticker

six pools · scrolling detail line

Six single-row stacked bars, one per pool, each with a two-LED status key at the left edge. Proportion is the message: how much of a pool has left nominal, and which pool it is. The lower half is a scrolling line naming the current findings in the same word order the console uses.

1C

Reachability waterfall

11 targets × 53 samples · time-shared text

The only face that shows history: eleven probed targets down the rows, the last nine minutes of samples scrolling right to left. Latency is brightness, loss is red, so a flapping path leaves a visible scar. Every twelve seconds the panel time-shares — the lattice drops away and the p95 and loss figures take the full board for three seconds.

1D

Ambient field

abstract by default · text on change

A slow azure field whose drift rate is aggregate load and whose agitation is alert pressure — furniture when all is well, with the clock and system count cycling through a cleared window in the middle. A fault stains the field from the edge nearest the affected pool and pins the offending name in place until it is acknowledged.
