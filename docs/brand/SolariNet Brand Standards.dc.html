Section 01 · Foundation

Background & principles

SolariNet is an open-source scheduling tool maintained by four people and used by roughly two hundred community projects. It exists because volunteer coordination was happening in spreadsheets that nobody owned.

This document sets the rules for how SolariNet looks and sounds, so that a contributor who has never spoken to the maintainers can produce work that fits.

01

Legible before beautiful

If a choice makes something prettier and harder to read, it loses.

02

One accent, held back

Colour marks the single most important thing on a surface, never the fifth.

03

Made to be forked

Every asset ships in an editable format with a licence attached.

Section 04 · Practice

Imagery

People at work
Candid, mid-action, never posed.
Detail
Hands, screens, objects. Tight crop.
Context
Wide, structural, low saturation subject.
Treatment
Grayscale, contrast +8%. No tints, no duotones, no filters.
Crop
3:2 or 1:1. Full-bleed only when the image is the page.
Sourcing
CC0 or contributor-supplied with written permission. Record the licence next to the file.
Never
Stock handshakes, generated faces, screenshots as decoration.

Section 04 · Practice

Voice & tone

Direct, not blunt

Say the thing in the first sentence. Then soften the edges, not the meaning.

Plain, not simple

Short words for hard ideas. We never write down to the reader.

Warm, not chummy

We thank people by name. We do not perform enthusiasm.

Situation
Tone
Sounds like
Onboarding
Encouraging, brief
“Three steps and you're scheduling. Start here.”
Errors
Plain, accountable
“We couldn't save that. Your draft is still here.”
Release notes
Factual, credited
“Recurring shifts now copy forward. Thanks, @rjc.”
Bad news
Early, specific
“The import tool is being retired in March. Here's the migration path.”

Section 04 · Practice

Writing rules

Don't
Do
“Oops! Something went wrong 😅”
“That shift didn't save — the server timed out.”
“Leverage SolariNet to optimise your scheduling workflow.”
“SolariNet builds the rota so you don't have to.”
“Users may optionally configure notifications.”
“You can turn notifications off.”
“Click HERE to learn more!!!”
“Read how recurring shifts work.”
Case
Sentence case everywhere, including buttons.
Numbers
Numerals from 10 up; spell zero to nine.
Punctuation
Oxford comma. One exclamation mark per release.
The name
“SolariNet”, never “SOLARINET” or “solarinet” in prose.

Section 04 · Practice

Motion

Instant
Hover, focus, toggle
100ms
Standard
Panels, menus, expansion
200ms
Deliberate
Page and route changes
320ms
Easing
One curve for everything
cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)
Reduced motion

Honour prefers-reduced-motion. Transitions drop to an opacity fade at 100ms; nothing moves, nothing parallaxes.

Motion has a job

Animate only to show where something came from or where it went. Decoration is not a job.

Never
Bounce, elastic, or overshoot easingLooping ambient animationScroll-jacking or parallaxAnything longer than 400ms
