1. Delivery contract

The emitter advances its checkpoint only after mandatory routing and broker confirmation. The DNS consumer renders current SoR state into a private directory and first verifies the Xenon release. It then refreshes the generated secondary-zone list on every configured secondary and waits for SOA parity across all generated zones. It acknowledges only after each target proves serving and its local status record is durable. An unchanged primary does not suppress a secondary retry.

Target work runs in a worker while the RabbitMQ owner thread handles heartbeats. After connection loss, the in-flight operation finishes before a retry starts. Startup and hourly reconciliation recover drift or missed events. The emitter checkpoint alone does not prove target freshness.

Primary success with a secondary failure is partial convergence: the event remains unacknowledged, that secondary stays failed/pending, and every configured secondary is attempted again. The ZimaBlade's future tertiary role is enabled only by adding a named secondary section after redeployment and independent service verification.

4. Recovery and retention

Failed activation: the helper revalidates the prior records, creates a rollback generation with serials newer than the attempted release, switches, reloads, and verifies serving. This allows secondaries that already transferred the failed generation to follow the rollback. Manually pointing at an older serial is insufficient.

Interrupted activation or failed rollback: the journal retains the previous and rollback identities. The next helper invocation retries recovery before new activation. The rollback generation is reused during retries. Removed/reintroduced zones retain serial history.

Interrupted bootstrap: re-run --bootstrap. It restores the exact legacy parent contents, mode, and ownership before attempting conversion again. Do not delete journals to bypass recovery. Keep previous generations, backups, and serial high-water marks until a reviewed retention policy covers all secondaries and replay sources.
